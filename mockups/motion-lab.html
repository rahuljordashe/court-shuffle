Court Shuffle · Motion Lab

Six ways to move a departure board.

Court Shuffle just got its first coat of motion in a parallel session: a staggered court reveal and a pulsing live dot. That sets a baseline; this lab settles the next decision, which motion system the app commits to. It puts six on the real UI so you can compare them on the same moment, side by side. Use the bar below to fire a moment, then watch all six respond. Each direction is a complete personality, not a menu of effects. They run from most restrained (1) to most theatrical (6). Pick one, or one for the hero moment and a quieter one for everything else.

How to read this: every direction respects prefers-reduced-motion (toggle it above to see the honest fallback: all six collapse to a 140 ms crossfade, no travel). Durations are tuned for a courtside tool: feedback 100–150 ms, reveals 240–320 ms, nothing over 420 ms. No bounce, no elastic, no confetti. All six preserve the data-testid hooks the Playwright suite keys off.
My recommendation Lead with 2 · Split-Flap for the hero moments (generate, scores, court count, ranks) because the Solari board is the brand North Star in DESIGN.md, and back it with 1 · Hairline discipline everywhere else (tabs, toasts, list edits). That pairing owns the metaphor without ever slowing the “generate, glance, go” path. If the split-flap reads as too much, 4 · Snap is the safe high-energy fallback.
